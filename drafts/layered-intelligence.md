# Layered Intelligence
Something I was musing about a few days ago was the news that we had achieved "AGI" or that these frontier models were somehow going to become the new skynet. 

I started to think about the problem more philosophically, what are the layers of intelligence bettween classical computers, ai, humans, and perhaps what lies beyond?

I generally came up with this as the scale:
1) **Mechanical** (Programs: Classical computation and Calculators)
2) **Pattern-Matching** (AI: Matrix/Vector Weighing and Prediction)
3) **Intuition** (Life: Novel real-world experience projected onto things)
4) **Abstraction** (Gods?: Relations between ideas that don't have realworld connections)
5+) **Beyond Human Conceivability** (If this tier exists, humans can't even fathom what it would be) 

This also led me to a few rules:
- An intelligence "thinks" using the mechanics of one layer only
- An intelligence can fully understand its own layer and those below it
- An intelligence is supremely best at its own native layer
- An intelligence is always exponentially slower (power for power) when downgrading its level
- An intelligence can comprhened the mode of intelligence one layer above it but not actually compute inside it
- An intelligence can only mock the computation of the layer above through its own lower-systems (analogies in our case)
- An intelligence can not even conceive of what would exist 2 layers above it, it can't even comprehend the mode
